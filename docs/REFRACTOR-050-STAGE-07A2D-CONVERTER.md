# Stage 07A.2-D — hybrid AC/DC converter, loss accounting, assumptions

**Status:** implementation candidate on branch, pending automated CI and live Home Assistant acceptance.

## Explicit opt-in
- `inverter.hybrid.enabled === true` plus `inverter.hybrid.converter = {schema_version:1,enabled:true,...}` are required. Legacy inverter configs remain AC-only with no converter, no reconnected edges and unchanged readings.
- `ac_port_id` identifies the AC boundary; `battery_port_id` identifies the **distinct** physical DC battery port. The literal `none` marks a deliberately absent DC battery; blank means missing required selection. `pv_mode:'mapped'` uses PV1–PV12 explicitly mapped MPPT meter observations; `pv_mode:'none'` represents deliberately absent PV. An explicit absence is known zero, but missing or invalid data are never zero.
- A DC battery participant must have explicit DC coupling, a valid selected port, known signed power and no ambiguous other participants or other active converter on its bus. Converter links do **not** merge physical AC/DC graphs or rewrite user-drawn cables.
- Sign at external converter boundaries (injection into network): PV MPPT **negative**; inverter battery DC **positive when charging** to supply the battery circuit and **negative when discharging**; inverter AC **positive export**, **negative import**. Power conservation for the device is `PV_dc - battery_charge_terminal - AC_export = loss >= 0` (with charge negative when discharging). Known raw AC power is not overwritten by matching-looking PV estimates.
- With known terminal powers, derive total loss and observed aggregate efficiency, rejecting negative loss beyond configured tolerance. This does not claim that observed loss is solely conversion heat: meter placement, auxiliary consumption and sample timing remain uncertainty.
- If AC power is missing or the user explicitly chooses calculate, estimate it only when PV and battery powers are complete and **both applicable efficiency assumptions** are provided. `efficiency_percent` is the DC/AC path assumption, and for nonzero battery power `battery_efficiency_percent` is the separate DC/DC path assumption. No implied 100% efficiency. Both accept explicit `100` if the user deliberately wishes to model an ideal path.
- Estimated AC: `DC_net = PV_in + discharge_at_battery × η_battery - charge_at_battery / η_battery`; if net >= 0, `AC = DC_net × η_AC`, otherwise `AC = DC_net / η_AC`. Actual measurements remain `measured` or `simulated` while estimates are `estimated`.
- No battery-source allocation or AC estimate is made if port mapping, units, readings, battery/DC topology or direction is ambiguous. Device-rated AC output exceeding the configured limit and measured output exceeding input create explicit errors. Neither error is silently overridden to force zero residual.
- An activated converter contributes exactly one internal battery-side signed injection, **not** an extra standalone battery load or independent PV source. Existing AC and DC topological networks remain separate. A converter without fully verified constraints cannot yield a known DC cable flow. No automatic electrical wiring, read-write HA calls, EPS switching, islanding or export-limit enforcement.

## Acceptance scenarios
Automated test `dev/test-hybrid-converter.js` covers PV→AC (5→4.8 kW), AC→battery (3.3 in→3 out, 0.3 loss), battery→AC, PV+charge+AC, 3.04 PV+1.5 charge with an explicit 96% AC and 100% battery efficiency (= 1.4784 AC), 96%/96% assumptions (=1.4184 AC), positive/negative/zero, incomplete data and both types of missing efficiency, measured inconsistency (AC 5 kW with insufficient DC source), rated power, unwired MPPT observation with opt-in mapping, unchanged original AC-only behavior and save/reload.

Real browser test in Home Assistant is mandatory: enable hybrid converter, select actual AC and DC battery ports, map MPPT input, set battery coupling DC and ensure its cable is attached. Verify PV 3.04, battery charge 1.50 and AC estimated 1.4784 only after *both* explicit efficiencies (AC 96%, battery 100%) are set. Leaving either required assumption blank must keep the estimated value missing. Change AC to an impossible 5 kW measurement and verify converter marks the conflict rather than declaring balance OK. No assumption can claim electricity was physically delivered.

## Explicitly deferred
EPS/islanding (07A.2-E), export limits (07A.2-F) and full integrated field tests/performance (07A.2-G) are separate. The traditional inverter SVG's own AC meter still displays its configured source until a future harmonized shared data/renderer integration; converter-estimated values are reported in balance diagnostics, not presented as measured HA data. Avoid announcing a stable release before HA acceptance.
